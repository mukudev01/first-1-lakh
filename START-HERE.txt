KUBER BOUTIQUE — START HERE
============================

This is the Savana-inspired Kuber Boutique storefront with your supplied photos
already added to product cards and category tiles. It includes sample INR prices,
size selection, search, and a customer order form. The visual direction takes
inspiration from Savana; the branding and page design are original.

OPEN THE STORE
--------------
Open index.html or kuber.html in a web browser. Keep the images folder next to
those files so the photos load. The full project is included in this download.

CHANGE PRODUCTS, CATEGORIES OR PRICES
-------------------------------------
1. Open script.js in a text editor.
2. Find the PRODUCTS list near the top.
3. Edit name, category, price, compareAt, tag, sizes, and image for each item.
   Prices are sample values in rupees. Change them to your actual prices.
4. If you connect Google Sheets, update the matching product name, category,
   price and SKU in google-apps-script.gs too.

CHANGE A PHOTO
--------------
Product images are already in the images folder with simple filenames. In
script.js, change a product's image path, for example:
image:"images/saree-mustard-front.png"
Keep the image file inside images. For extra product photos, add another entry
to the PRODUCTS list and give it a unique SKU. The optional imageBack field is
used for the hover image when a second view is available.

CONNECT ORDERS TO A GOOGLE SHEET AND PAYMENT PAGE
------------------------------------------------
1. Create a Google Sheet for boutique orders.
2. In the Sheet choose Extensions > Apps Script. Paste in google-apps-script.gs.
3. Replace PASTE_GOOGLE_SHEET_ID_HERE with the ID from the Sheet's address bar.
4. Add each hosted payment URL beside its product in ORDER_PRODUCTS. Create these
   links in your chosen payment provider and set each amount to the same price.
5. In Apps Script choose Deploy > New deployment > Web app. Set “Execute as” to
   yourself and access to “Anyone”, then deploy. Google will ask you to approve
   the script's access to your Sheet.
6. Copy the deployed URL ending in /exec and paste it into orderEndpoint near the
   top of script.js.
7. Keep the product SKUs, names and prices consistent in script.js and the Apps
   Script product list.

Until you complete that setup, the form is a preview and doesn't save customer
information or take payment. Verify payments in your provider account before
shipping. Update the sample delivery, exchange, and contact wording in kuber.html
to match your store.
