KUBER BOUTIQUE — START HERE
============================

This is a Savana-inspired boutique storefront starter: clean category browsing,
large editorial product cards, filters, search, sample INR prices, and an order
form shown before payment. The visual direction is inspired by the reference;
the branding and layout are original for Kuber Boutique.

FILES
-----
index.html              The page structure.
styles.css              Colours, layout, typography and mobile styling.
script.js               Sample categories, products, prices, images and order URL.
google-apps-script.gs   Optional small Google Sheets order receiver.

HOW TO CHANGE PRODUCTS AND PRICES
---------------------------------
1. Open script.js in a text editor.
2. Find the PRODUCTS list near the top.
3. Change name, category, price, compareAt, tag and sizes for each item.
   Prices are sample values in rupees. compareAt is the crossed-out price.
4. Keep each product's sku unique. Copy the same SKU, name, category and price
   into ORDER_PRODUCTS in google-apps-script.gs if you connect the Sheet.

HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
----------------------
1. Create a folder called images beside index.html.
2. Put your product photo files in that folder.
3. For that product in script.js, replace image:"" with e.g.
   image:"images/paithani-saree.jpg"
4. Use a vertical photo when possible. The product cards crop photos to fit.
   Until you add photos, the cards show a coordinated illustrated placeholder.

HOW TO CONNECT ORDERS TO A GOOGLE SHEET
---------------------------------------
1. Create a Google Sheet for boutique orders.
2. In that Sheet choose Extensions > Apps Script.
3. Paste in the contents of google-apps-script.gs. Replace
   PASTE_GOOGLE_SHEET_ID_HERE with the ID in your Sheet's address bar.
4. In ORDER_PRODUCTS, paste the matching hosted payment URL into paymentUrl for
   each product. The payment link must charge the same price as the product.
5. In Apps Script choose Deploy > New deployment > Web app. Set “Execute as” to
   yourself and access to “Anyone”, then deploy and copy the URL ending in /exec.
   Google will ask you to authorize this script to add rows to your Sheet.
6. Paste the URL into orderEndpoint near the top of script.js.
7. Keep the product prices and payment-link amounts in sync. The order amount is
   set in the Apps Script product list; the provider's hosted page takes payment.

BEFORE YOU TAKE REAL ORDERS
---------------------------
- The site is a static front end. The order form currently runs in preview mode
  and sends or saves no details until you connect the Google Apps Script URL.
- A payment link is also a sample until you add your own provider URLs in the
  Apps Script product list. Use the provider's dashboard to verify payment before
  dispatching an order.
- Check that you can access your Google Sheet and Apps Script deployment from a
  different browser or phone before sharing the storefront with customers.
- Update the sample delivery, exchange and contact wording in index.html to match
  your actual store policies and contact details.

TO OPEN THE SITE
----------------
Open index.html in a web browser. You can preview the design without setting up
the Google Sheet or payment links. Google Fonts need an internet connection;
the rest of the page is included in this folder.
